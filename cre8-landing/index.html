Before you decide

Questions buyers ask us

What's the difference between Type A, B and C?

Type A (4 units) are intermediate factories on 0.99 acres with 22,833 sq ft built-up. Type B (8 units) are intermediate factories on 1.20–1.24 acres with 25,865 sq ft built-up and side ramps. Type C (9 units) are corner factories on 1.19–1.25 acres with 25,844 sq ft built-up.

How big are the factories?

Land areas range from 43,056 to 54,335 sq ft (0.99–1.25 acres); built-up areas range from 22,833 to 25,865 sq ft. Each has a 12-metre ceiling height and 3-tonne floor loading on the production floor.

What's inside each factory?

A ground-level production area, offices on the ground and first floors, a canteen, washrooms and a surau, with loading access at the front. Refer to the lot plans for exact layouts — we'll walk you through them at the viewing.

When will the Bukit Beruntung access be ready?

The planned future connection within Novva Industrial Park towards Bukit Beruntung Interchange is targeted for Q1 2028. This is subject to infrastructure implementation and authority / developer progress. Sungai Buaya Interchange access is available today.

How much does a factory cost?

SPA prices start from RM 9,985,000 (Type A). Prices differ by lot — see each lot in the selection above, or ask us for the full price chart.

What does "PE" mean on some lots?

Some lots are marked "PE" on the site plan. Ask our team during your viewing and we'll show you exactly what it means for that lot.

Can I visit the site?

Yes — that's the best way to decide. Book a private viewing below and our team will meet you, walk you through the site, plans and lots, and answer every question.
